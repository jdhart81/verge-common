# Service coverage and remaining work

Initially reviewed against source revision `baa2ffd` on 19 September 2026 and updated for the continuing, unreleased build below. This is a claim-to-implementation audit, not a deployment receipt or evidence of real conservation activity. Production was not inspected for this audit; the current live revision must be checked separately before describing a source feature as available on the website.

The homepage's present service is community coordination and conservation preparation: bring neighbors together, map contributions, document consent, work with a willing partner, record field work, and make cooperative decisions. Its carbon-income answer explicitly leaves eligibility, verification, issuance, sales and payments to external parties. Source supports much of that bounded service. It does not yet support the broader end-to-end operating model described in [COOPERATIVE_SYSTEM.md](COOPERATIVE_SYSTEM.md), particularly continuing obligations and complete financial reconciliation.

## Continuing build

- The workspace now refreshes while visible and online without remounting same-workspace forms, pauses reads around saves, and displays a stale-record warning after a background failure. [Refresh controller](../lib/workspace-refresh.mjs), [workspace loader](../components/network-app.tsx).
- The pending-work queue now includes observations, imagery results, holdings, settlements, draft allocations, member-payment receipts, stewardship/treasury receipts and retirements. Authenticated server projections determine whether the viewer can independently review each record; missing flags fail closed. New notices reveal only counts, category labels and the appropriate existing tab. The focused pending-work/partner suite passed **13 tests**, including actual domain transitions for all eight new categories, self-review exclusion, ordinary-member projections, paused capacity, redacted finance and archival. [Queue tests](../tests/pending-work.test.mjs).
- Stewardship/treasury receipt recording and the native reply client now have candidate implementations. Receipt cards expose the exact supporting evidence only when it is in the viewer's authorized projection; capacity-paused workspaces retain historical totals but cannot add financial records. The [service build receipt](SERVICE_BUILD_2026-09-19.md) records the implementation scope and must retain final test, build and hosted evidence; source presence is not deployment or Apple distribution.

## Remaining priority work with concrete acceptance

1. **Close the ongoing conservation-care loop.** Add a project/parcel-linked obligation and issue record with responsible person, due date, source agreement/method reference, evidence, status history and independent closure where needed. Include disturbances/reversal concerns without automatically changing registry units or declaring legal compliance. Verify access boundaries, overdue work, changed/withdrawn boundaries, member exit and retained obligations. Existing tasks can help coordinate activity; they currently have only a title, optional due string, assignee and completion status. Source entry points: [task/observation commands](../lib/network.mjs), [monitoring board](../components/monitoring-board.tsx).
2. **Finish the limits of financial reconciliation before recording complex real proceeds.** Specify partial member payments, refunds, correction/reversal history and settlement fee/adjustment treatment. Preserve immutable original records, independent review and exact minor-unit balances. The new stewardship/treasury receipt path closes an allocation-bucket gap; it does not establish a full accounting or payment-execution system. Source entry points: [allocation/payment commands](../lib/network.mjs), the Finance panel in [workspace UI](../components/network-app.tsx).
3. **Qualify the actual service release.** Complete candidate checks and hosted multi-account acceptance, verify the deployed revision and recovery state, and rehearse the workflow with consenting participants. Verify that a neighbor's change arrives without discarding drafts, access revocation clears private records, hidden/blocked content stays hidden, review queues clear after decisions, and the native retry queue cannot duplicate or cross-post replies between accounts. Outbound email/push remains explicitly absent until a delivery service is selected and tested.

General project decisions and consented partner document sharing should follow if pilot participants need them; neither requires opening private records to every group member. Automatic credit trading, carbon quantification and bank payouts require a separate selected external program/provider and real-world qualification. They cannot be completed by adding a “sell” button to an internal ledger.
