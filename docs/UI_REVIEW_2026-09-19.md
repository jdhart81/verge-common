# Mission and interface review — September 19, 2026

The homepage now leads with participation in conservation. Starting a co-op and exploring the community are the primary actions; the local planner remains a no-account option, and source/contributor access is retained. The mission headline is unchanged.

## Findings addressed

- The primary action previously sent neighbors to GitHub despite working shared co-ops. The new entry flow goes directly to registration with the intended workspace destination.
- A text-only field note did not explain the relationship between neighboring land, shared care and a conservation nonprofit. A custom SVG landscape now depicts those relationships, using parcels, connected habitat, homes, a creek, a local partner and field notes. It is labeled as an illustration, not actual land or evidence.
- Dense feature descriptions have become three practical steps, a shared-workspace explanation, and expandable answers about participation, pooling, partners and carbon preparation. Carbon income is a possible external pathway, never promised or presented as automatic.
- The homepage used conflicting contributor-preview/pilot language. The hosted page now consistently describes an early community pilot; the separate static preview still explains its limited local behavior.
- Account links lost the co-op destination when switching to registration or recovery. Mode links, forms and recovery completion now preserve the validated destination. External destinations remain rejected.
- Account setup exposed optional integration jargon too early. Device/agent controls are collapsed, recovery guidance is prominent, and inputs have plain-language requirements and accessible descriptions.
- Filtering a populated discovery list to zero matches previously produced a blank area. It now shows a no-match explanation and a clear-search action.

## Motion and access

The SVG uses local vector shapes and CSS; no animation library, third-party image, tracking request or new dependency is required. The full illustration remains visible when still. Motion is gentle, pausable using a visible keyboard-operable control, disabled by default for reduced-motion preferences, and paused when the figure is outside the viewport. A visitor can explicitly choose to play it. Its accessible title/description explains the complete concept without relying on motion or color alone.

The homepage uses semantic sections, a skip link, visible keyboard focus, native disclosure controls and mobile layouts. Account requirements are associated with their inputs. This is a focused interface review, not an independent accessibility certification or real-participant usability study.

- Self-hosted and separate static-preview builds passed.
- The production-build multi-account acceptance suite passed against isolated local data, including the homepage/app route distinction, invitations, permissions, files, native tokens and hosted MCP.
