# VergeCommon v0.9.0 deployment — 29 September 2026 EDT

Deployed **30 September 2026 at 01:36:12 UTC (29 September at 21:36:12 EDT)** under Justin's instruction to finish the mission tooling and ship it. The production release is tagged source `v0.9.0`, not the later documentation commit.

## Delivered behavior

- Public project, event and progress pages with social previews, share/copy controls and QR codes. Sign-in and membership requests preserve the chosen activity; private meeting instructions and evidence remain private.
- Actual attendance separate from RSVP, organizer completion records, independent steward review, and explicit public-result publication/withdrawal.
- Assigned recurring conservation care and disturbance records, evidence submission, independent review and a continuing history.
- A private **Care & participation** workspace tab with notices, care workflows and a participation report. Founder, test and unclassified accounts are excluded from participant totals; repeat contribution and contributing shared-link recipients are separate measures.
- Opt-in browser reminders, generic encrypted messages, subscription expiry handling and account-erasure cleanup. Reminders are off by default. No production subscriptions existed at final inspection; actual device display remains unqualified.
- Account erasure and public-visibility protections for all new records; dependency updates with a clean vulnerability audit.

These tools support a real conservation pilot. They do not certify ecological outcomes, issue carbon credits, move money, guarantee viral growth, or complete native mobile distribution.

## Rollback compatibility

Run the new image's `self-hosted/rollback-check.mjs /data` against a read-only live-data mount before restarting any pre-v0.9.0 writer. The guard refuses when new care, event-result or activity/referrer records exist because an older writer cannot safely preserve or erase them. In that case retain current data and ship a compatible/forward repair. Never run two live-data writers, and never restore an older backup over new records. The retained image is recovery material, not permission to bypass this guard.

Local verification receipts and synthetic UI screenshots are retained in the ignored `outputs/v090-release/` directory. Production database contents and secrets remain on the host.
