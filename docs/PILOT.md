# First community pilot

Use consenting participants and real conservation work. Do not publish their names, parcel references, or meeting instructions without appropriate consent. The shared website is live at https://vergecommon.com. Participants create their own accounts; a private invitation still requires steward approval before it grants co-op record access. Begin with a bounded, consenting pilot and the current deployment/recovery limits.

## Organizer preparation

- Appoint two stewards and agree who handles access requests, conduct concerns, and urgent event changes.
- Choose the co-op country/currency before recording money; explain that app membership does not itself create legal or financial rights.
- Decide which introductions, projects, and event summaries may be public. Keep precise locations in private meeting instructions.
- Create one useful activity and a clear description of what participants will do.

## Participation acceptance

1. An invited person signs in, uses the private link, and submits a request. Before approval they cannot read co-op records.
2. A steward approves the member. The person can find the community board, respond to an event, and understand the displayed time zone.
3. A second person tests event capacity, changes their RSVP, and downloads a calendar entry. Explain that downloads do not receive automatic cancellation updates.
4. A member posts an update and another replies. Replies remain member-only even when the original update is public.
5. A participant reports synthetic test content. Another steward resolves it, and hidden content disappears from ordinary member/public views.
6. A member loses access after removal. The organizer confirms retained historical records do not imply current membership.
7. Hold the actual activity. Record completed work with evidence appropriate to the claim; an RSVP alone is not attendance.
8. Invite participants back for a second useful activity. Record whether they returned and what prevented participation.

## Partner and evidence acceptance

- Have the organization's consenting contact join as an ordinary member. Record and independently review the partnership agreement, then invite that contact from Organizations. Confirm the person can see the invitation without access to the private agreement file or other members' evidence.
- The representative accepts with their role and an authority reference, submits their own supporting evidence, and another steward reviews the evidence and participation. Confirm this grants no extra permissions and is described as the co-op's review, not institutional verification or a signed agreement. Test declining and ending participation.
- On the qualified native beta build, choose a nonsensitive photo and a PDF/text sample. Check the destination before submitting. Interrupt the connection, retry the same pending attempt, and confirm one uploaded file and one evidence record. Test account switching, cancellation and explicit local cleanup. Photo metadata should be absent from the received reencoded file; PDF/text must be reviewed by the person because their contents and metadata are preserved.
- On an isolated synthetic co-op, reach the displayed capacity warning and archive it. Confirm ordinary additions pause while authorized reports, access removal, consent withdrawal and participation ending remain available within the finite safety reserve. A real co-op approaching the hard limit needs operator intervention; do not fill a live co-op for this test.

## Evidence to record

Track the number of invited people, approved members, event responses, confirmed attendance documented by the organizer, completed useful actions, returning participants, unresolved concerns, and participant-originated referrals. Keep the period and counting method with each figure. Counts in the community board are software records, not independently verified attendance or environmental impact.

## Stop and fix

Pause broader rollout if private data is exposed, approvals are bypassed, members cannot report problems, financial records do not reconcile, or participants cannot understand what rights and obligations they are accepting. Never use a software score to bypass local legal review or external carbon verification.

A private link is copied/shared by an organizer, not automatically emailed. Joining through login, registration or recovery stays in the same tab; store the new recovery code securely. If session storage is blocked or the invitation is unavailable, reopen the original link after authentication and ask the organizer for help with an expired/revoked link. A request still needs steward approval; pending invitees have no private record access. Account recovery rotates the recovery code and revokes previous sessions/tokens. Without a working login or recovery code, use the confirmed private support process; never send passwords, recovery codes or tokens in a report.

Two active stewards support member decisions; their roles do not grant technical operator access. Removed members lose private access; retained historical records do not grant membership. Keep exact meeting instructions, evidence and precise land/participant records private. Publish only an explicitly consented summary after independent completed-work review, and use the existing withdrawal controls if consent changes. A complaint about the founding steward can use independent `/report/` intake, not only a steward's review. Save the private report receipt to check its general status; support coverage is not promised until the owner confirms it. Urgent threats need the agreed external response process.

## Urgent event changes and calendar limitations

Before invitations, agree on an already available **consented** phone/text/email/group channel that reaches every participant, the person who sends changes, their backup, acknowledgement expectations and fallback for anyone not reached. This is an owner choice, not a new integration. Keep contact details and the checklist in their approved private location; the app has no new contact field.

1. Save a schedule/location change or cancellation through the existing authorized event workflow. Refresh after a conflict and check current details; do not repeat an uncertain save as a new request.
2. Notify affected Going **and Interested** participants and organizer-known attendees through the agreed channel. A public viewer is not a known reachable attendee. Existing RSVPs are retained and capacity cannot evict existing Going responses.
3. Privately record when the message was sent, who acknowledged and the nonresponder fallback taken. A minimal external checklist is enough: event/version reference, sender/backup, send time, acknowledgement state, fallback/stop decision. Do not put contacts or acknowledgements in public evidence or the readiness manifest. A checkbox, calendar export or push request does not prove delivery.
4. Ask participants to view current details and re-download/import the latest calendar entry. Files keep a stable UID/incremented sequence but are **not subscriptions**; client import behavior varies. Do not promise automatic cancellation or removal from their calendar.
5. If urgent changes cannot reliably reach participants, postpone/stop the activity or use the agreed fallback. Record the decision privately.

Browser reminders are supplementary, opt-in per device and off by default. No opt-in is required to join. Private notices and reminders can be throttled (event updates currently eight hours; digest seven days). A provider-accepted request does not prove device display or human receipt. Test actual display on every opted-in participating device/browser; denied, unsupported, offline and accepted-but-not-displayed states use the agreed channel/current-details fallback. Test sign-out/account switching, stale subscriptions, removal and account deletion without exposing another account's reminders. Installed iPhone/iPad web apps and VoiceOver/real-device checks remain unverified until performed.

## Post-pilot evaluation (BL-13)

After activities occur, use existing Care & participation reports plus the organizer's attendance record. For each count of invited/approved/RSVP/actual attendance/completed work/returns/referrals, record the period, counting method and owner/synthetic/test/independent-participant classification. Do not count test accounts as independent adoption. State concerns, unresolved reports and reasons for nonreturn; preserve participant privacy. Real useful work and return participation guide the next decision. Source code, passing checks, traffic and software counts do not establish ecological impact. BL-13 completion is not a gate for starting the first pilot; a wider announcement requires separate approval.
