# Community conduct

VergeCommon welcomes people working to care for shared places, with or without technical experience. Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Critique ideas and evidence, not people. Make room for local knowledge and different levels of experience.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and publishing private locations or participant records are unacceptable. Obtain permission before sharing another person's work or information. Use synthetic data in examples and reports.

Justin Hart, the initial maintainer, handles conduct concerns and may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, or restrict participation. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior and its impact. Contributors may ask for a decision to be reconsidered with relevant context.

For a concern that can be described safely in public, open an issue without naming affected people or exposing sensitive details. Otherwise request a private contact without publishing the incident. A dedicated confidential conduct channel has not yet been established; it is a public-community launch gate in [the launch checklist](docs/LAUNCH.md). Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
